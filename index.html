Privacy Policy for MP3 Vision

Effective Date: November 24, 2026
Developer: BPHE Studio

BPHE Studio ("we," "our," or "us") built MP3 Vision as a free, ad-free music player application. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use MP3 Vision.

1. Local Media & Privacy-First Design

  • Audio, Video, and Subtitle Files: All .mp3, .mp4, .vtt lyric files, and custom playlist cover images you import into MP3 Vision are stored locally on your device (using your browser or device's local storage and IndexedDB).
  • Song Titles Stay Private: Your local audio files and song titles are never uploaded to our servers.

2. Information We Collect (Optional Cloud Sync)

You can use MP3 Vision completely offline without creating an account. If you choose to create an optional account to sync your listening statistics across devices, we collect and store the following data via Google Firebase:

  • Account Information: Your email address and encrypted authentication credentials (managed by Firebase Authentication).
  • Device Identifiers: A randomly generated device ID and the custom device name you provide (e.g., "Android Phone" or "Web Player") to enable cross-device playback resumption.
  • Listening Activity & Preferences: Play counts, listening durations, daily and weekly listening statistics, artist names, custom playlists, saved lyrics, and player settings (shuffle and loop states).

3. Third-Party Services

MP3 Vision uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Realtime Database): Used strictly to authenticate optional user accounts and sync listening statistics and playlists across your devices. You can review Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • PayPal: MP3 Vision includes an optional external link (paypal.me/BPHEStudio) for voluntary donations. If you choose to donate, your transaction is handled directly by PayPal under their Privacy Policy at paypal.com/webapps/mpp/ua/privacy-full. We do not receive or store your financial or payment card details.

4. Advertising & Tracking

  • No Ads: MP3 Vision contains zero advertisements and does not integrate any third-party advertising networks or ad-tracking SDKs.
  • No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

  • Local Data: You can delete your local songs, playlists, and cached files at any time within the app or by clearing your app or browser storage.
  • Cloud Data: If you created an optional sync account, we retain your cloud-synced statistics until you request deletion.

How to Delete Your Account: Click the button below to open a pre-filled deletion request email to lalalalalaronald@gmail.com. We will permanently delete your Firebase account and all associated cloud-synced data within 7 days.

6. Children's Privacy

MP3 Vision is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
